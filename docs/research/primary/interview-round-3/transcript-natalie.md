# Feelio Prototype Evaluation Transcript

**Facilitator:** Hi, thank you for participating in our prototype evaluation today. During this session, I will ask you some questions and invite you to complete several tasks.

Please use the think-aloud method by describing what you are looking at, what you think each element means, and what you expect to happen. Do you have any questions before we begin?

**Participant:** No, we can start.

---

## Task 1: Select and Send an Emotion

**Facilitator:** Imagine that you have had a stressful day. Your partner does not know what happened, but you want them to know how you are currently feeling before they contact you.

Using Feelio, please select “stressed” as your current emotion and send it to your partner. Please remember to think aloud while completing this task. What would you do first?

**Participant:** I want to select the emotion that represents my current mood. I would press the right arm to change the emotion. Pressing it once changes the emotion, so I would continue until I reach the stressed facial expression.

**Facilitator:** What would you do next?

**Participant:** I would double-press the button to send the selected “stressed” emotion.

**Facilitator:** Was there any feedback or information you expected but did not receive, or was everything clear?

**Participant:** I assume that when I change the emotion, the facial expression on the screen also changes. That would let me know which emotion I have selected.

**Facilitator:** Yes, the facial expression would change.

**Participant:** Once I see that the face has changed to the stressed expression, I would know that I had selected the correct emotion and could send it.

---

## Task 2: Identify Your Partner’s Emotion

**Facilitator:** Imagine that you have not seen your partner for the entire day, and you want to know how they are currently feeling.

Please find and identify your partner’s current emotion. Once you have identified it, explain whether this information would affect how and when you approach your partner.

**Participant:** To see my partner’s emotion, I would flip open the belly cover. I would then see the facial expression they selected, which would tell me how they are feeling.

**Facilitator:** Would this information help you decide how or when to approach your partner?

**Participant:** A facial expression alone would not provide the full context. I would also want to understand their tone and the intensity of their emotion.

Since the instructions say that I can press the left arm to hear their voice message, I would also do that. I would listen to what they said and their tone of voice to better understand their mood.

The visual cue would give me an initial understanding of what they were feeling, but it would not tell me the intensity. For example, if they selected “stressed,” I would not know whether they were slightly stressed, moderately stressed, or extremely stressed.

By listening to the recording and their tone of voice, I could better understand how stressed they were.

**Facilitator:** So, after seeing the emotion, you would also listen to the audio message to understand the context?

**Participant:** Yes, I would want to understand the context as well.

**Facilitator:** Imagine that your partner selected this angry expression. Is it clear which emotion they are experiencing?

**Participant:** Initially, it is clear that they are angry. However, I would want to know what they are angry about. Are they angry at me, or are they angry because of something else that happened in their life?

**Facilitator:** After discovering that your partner is angry, what would you do next? Would it change how or when you approach them?

**Participant:** I think I would talk to them immediately. I would probably send them a voice message.

---

## Task 3: Record and Send a Voice Message

**Facilitator:** Imagine that the emotion “stressed” does not fully explain what happened. You want to give your partner additional context without writing a long message.

Please use Feelio to record and send a short voice message to your partner.

**Participant:** I would press and hold the button on the back to begin recording. I would explain how I was feeling and why I was stressed.

After that, I would stop the recording. However, I am not sure what I would need to do to send it. Would I press the right arm to send it? If I release the recording button, would it automatically send the message, or would I need to press another button?

**Facilitator:** So, this part is not clear enough for you?

**Participant:** Yes. The first step is clear: I would press and hold the button to record. When I release the button, I would expect the recording to stop.

However, I would not know whether the message had already been sent or whether I needed to press another button to send it.

---

## Task 4: Listen to a Voice Message

**Facilitator:** Imagine that your partner has sent you a voice message. Please use Feelio to find and listen to the message.

**Participant:** I would press the left-arm button once to play the message, and I would listen to it through the speaker on the back.

**Facilitator:** Is there any additional feedback that would help you know whether the message is playing, paused, or finished?

**Participant:** I would assume that pressing the button once would play the recording one time and that it would stop when it reached the end.

Another possible interaction would be pressing and holding the button to play the recording. The recording could continue while I hold the button and stop whenever I release it.

---

## Helmet and Privacy Feature

**Facilitator:** What do you think about this helmet?

**Participant:** It is interesting. Is it a privacy helmet? Does it have a particular function?

**Facilitator:** It covers your emotion when you need privacy. What was your initial impression?

**Participant:** I thought it would cover or hide how I was feeling. However, if I cover the face, can my partner still see my emotion through the belly screen?

**Facilitator:** Yes, your partner can still see your emotion.

**Participant:** So, it only hides the emotion on my own device?

**Facilitator:** Feelio is a keychain that you may carry with you. The helmet prevents other people around you from seeing the emotion displayed on its face.

**Participant:** That makes sense. It would stop other people from seeing how I am feeling. It also looks visually appealing and gives the device a different appearance.

---

## Overall Experience

**Facilitator:** Do you think Feelio could influence how or when you approach your partner?

**Participant:** I think it would be helpful for people in long-distance relationships, which is something I am currently experiencing.

I would use it because I do not get to see my partner all the time. Seeing their emotional status could reassure me that they are fine and safe.

The voice-recording feature would also be useful. Instead of making a real-time phone call, we could send each other short voice messages. It would feel a little like sending a voice-recorded letter.

---

## Unnecessary or Complicated Features

**Facilitator:** Did any feature feel unnecessary or too complicated?

**Participant:** I think everything was relatively straightforward. My main concern is the recording interaction.

When I release the recording button, does the message send automatically? Would I hear a notification sound or receive another form of feedback confirming that it had been sent? Alternatively, would I need to press another button?

I would want an audio or visual cue confirming that the message had been successfully sent. Otherwise, I might think I had sent something when my partner had not actually received it. My partner might also become worried if they expected a message from me but did not receive anything.

A confirmation cue would reassure me that the message had been sent successfully.

---

## Suggestions for Improvement

**Facilitator:** Is there anything you would change or add to Feelio?

**Participant:** Overall, I think it is fine. However, you could provide a wider variety of emotions because emotions are almost limitless. There are many different emotions, so you could include more than the eight currently available.

**Facilitator:** Is that everything?

**Participant:** Yes.

**Facilitator:** Thank you for participating and sharing your feedback. Your feedback is very meaningful to us. I will stop the recording now.
