## 1. Introduction and Consent

**Facilitator:** Hello, thank you for participating in our prototype evaluation today.

We are testing a prototype called Feelio, which is designed to support emotional communication between couples.

Today, we are testing the prototype, not you. There are no right or wrong answers, and any difficulties you experience will help us improve the design.

During the session, I will ask you to complete several tasks. Please think aloud by describing what you are looking at, what you think each component does, and what you expect to happen.

I may remain quiet while you complete the tasks because we want to understand how you would use the prototype independently. If you become stuck, you can let me know.

With your permission, we would like to take notes and audio-record this session for research purposes. Your responses will remain confidential, and you may stop the session at any time.

Do you have any questions before we begin?

**Facilitator:** Okay, thank you.

---

## 2. Task One: Communicating Your Current Emotion

**Facilitator:** We're going to do Task One now.

Imagine that you have had a stressful day. Your partner does not know what has happened, but you want them to know how you are currently feeling before they contact you.

Using Feelio, please select "stressed" as your current emotion and send it to your partner.

Please remember to think aloud as you complete the task.

### Technical Difficulties and Contextual Summary

Due to technical difficulties with the audio recording, the participant's interaction during Task One was not fully captured.

During the task, the participant suggested that Feelio's hand buttons should function as left and right directional controls, rather than having the left hand switch between emotions and the right hand confirm a selection. She proposed placing a separate confirmation button elsewhere on the robot's body, allowing users to navigate emotions in both directions.

This preference was reiterated during the post-test interview.

## 6. Post-Test Interview

**Facilitator:** That's it for the tasks today. We're just going to ask you a few final post-test interview questions.

Thank you for completing all the tasks.

Do you think Feelio could influence how or when you would approach your partner?

**Participant:** Yeah, definitely. I think it would be helpful.

As long as the two partners set a boundary when they buy Feelio, so they don't abuse it.

Because I think there's a possibility for some couples to abuse it in the wrong way, or weaponise it.

**Facilitator:** Yeah, weaponise it.

**Facilitator:** Was there any feature that felt unnecessary or too complicated?

**Participant:** I still think the hand buttons should be left and right. It feels more intuitive.

I think the pause and confirm button should be somewhere else.

It would be annoying if, let's say, you wanted to choose a facial expression, but you accidentally pressed left instead of right.

Then you would have to keep pressing in one direction until you get to the emotion that you could have reached easily with the other direction button.

**Facilitator:** Right.

**Participant:** I would also like to compliment the character. It's very cute. I really like it.

**Facilitator:** Thank you, I appreciate that.

Last question: If you could change one thing about Feelio, what would it be?

**Participant:** The buttons, as I mentioned.

And some cute sound effects would be nice.

**Facilitator:** Okay.

Thank you for participating and sharing your feedback. Your responses will help us improve Feelio's physical interactions and understand whether it could support emotional communication between couples.

**Participant:** I also feel like some sounds that are very satisfying to listen to would be good.
