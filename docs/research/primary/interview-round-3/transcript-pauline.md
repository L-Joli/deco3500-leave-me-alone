# Feelio Usability Testing Interview Transcript

## Stage 2: Initial Exploration

**Scenario Prompt:**

> "Imagine that you have just received this product for the first time and have not been given any instructions. Before interacting with it, please examine the product and answer the following questions:"

### Q1: What do you think this product is designed to do?

**Participant:** For me personally, because I usually leave home quite early, I think this could be used to leave voice messages for my partner. It feels pretty romantic.

### Q2: What do you think each component does?

**[Follow-up]** What do you think the face display represents?

**Participant:** Someone's mood, but I can't tell whether it's my own emotion or my partner's.

**[Follow-up]** What do you think the two hand buttons might do?

**Participant:** It feels like pressing them would play some sound.

**[Follow-up]** What do you think the belly section is for?

**Participant:** Someone's emotion, but I'm not sure if it's mine or my partner's.

**[Follow-up]** What do you think the button on the back is for?

**Participant:** There's an obvious microphone icon; it looks like pressing it records audio.

**[Follow-up]** How would you play a received voice recording?

**Participant:** I would try pressing the buttons on the hands to see if they play the recording.

**[Follow-up]** What do you think the helmet accessory might do?

**Participant:** It feels like a phone case concept where you can switch up the style or look.

---

## Task 1: Select and Send an Emotion

**Scenario Prompt:**

> "Imagine that you have had a stressful day. Your partner does not know what has happened, but you want them to know how you are currently feeling before they contact you."

**Task Instruction:**

> "Using Feelio, please select 'Stressed' as your current emotion and send it to your partner. Please remember to think aloud as you complete the task."

### Q3: Think-Aloud during operation (User Observation)

**Participant:** There are only three buttons on the front, so I’d try pressing each one. I might try the right hand and notice the expression changes, which tells me it's related to switching emotions. But I don't know how to send it to the other person. I'd probably just keep pressing until I land on the emotion I want, and then stop there.

---

## Task 2: Find and Recognise the Partner’s Emotion

**Scenario Prompt:**

> "Imagine that you and your partner had planned to spend Saturday together. You had been looking forward to it, but your partner cancelled at the last minute to spend time with their friends. You feel disappointed and want to talk to your partner about how the cancellation affected you. Before starting the conversation, you want to understand how your partner is currently feeling."

**Task Instruction:**

> "Using Feelio, please find and identify your partner's current emotion. Once you have identified it, please explain whether this information would affect how or when you approach your partner."

### Q4: In this scenario, how would you use Feelio to find and recognise your partner's emotion?

**Participant:** I wouldn't use it. I would just directly ask my partner why they cancelled.

**[Follow-up]** Under the premise that you must check it, how would you use Feelio to view their emotion?

**Participant:** After recording a message, I would leave my Feelio on their desk. Since it can record and play audio, they could press play after I leave it there, and it would directly play the question I want to ask.

**[Follow-up]** Feelio allows you to check their emotion directly; how would you verify their current mood using it?

**Participant:** I would walk over and look at what expression their Feelio is currently displaying.

**[Follow-up]** If this single Feelio device can display both your emotion and your partner's emotion, how would you check their emotion?

**Participant:** When I was pressing earlier, only the top part moved, so I know the top controls my own emotion. I would try lifting the flap on the belly. Seeing that I can't control the emotion down there, I would deduce that the belly area displays my partner's expression.

---

## Task 3: Record and Send a Voice Message

**Scenario Prompt:**

> "Imagine that the emotion 'Stressed' does not fully explain what has happened. You want to give your partner a little more context without writing a long message."

**Task Instruction:**

> "Using Feelio, please record and send a short voice message to your partner. You may say anything you feel comfortable saying; the content of the message is not being evaluated."

### Q5: How would you operate the device to record and send the message?

**Participant:** I would press and hold the button until I finish recording.

### Q6: Did this interaction feel simple or complicated? Why?

**Participant:** This product makes the communication process with a partner more complicated, but the recording function itself is very intuitive.

---

## Task 4: Listen to a Voice Message

**Scenario Prompt:**

> "Imagine that your partner has sent you a voice message through Feelio."

**Task Instruction:**

> "Using Feelio, please find and listen to the voice message. Please think aloud and describe what you are looking for, what you are trying to do, and what you expect to happen."

### Q7: How would you locate and play the message?

**Participant:** The right hand controls emotions, so I would use the left button to play the recording. I'd press it once to see how it responds.

### Q8: What feedback would help you clearly know that the message is playing, paused, or finished?

**Participant:** An audio prompt or chime indicating that playback has started or finished would be helpful.

---

## Task 5: Use the Privacy Helmet

**Scenario Prompt:**

> "Imagine that you are in a public place. You still want your partner to receive your emotional updates, but you do not want other people nearby to see your current emotion."

**Task Instruction:**

> "Using the available accessories, please show me how you would keep your emotions private."

### Q9: What do you think about the design of this helmet?

**Participant:** I feel it's a bit redundant. It would be easier to just use an on/off toggle switch to decide whether to display the emotion or not.

**[Follow-up]** If there were an on/off mechanism, where would you expect to press to toggle the display on or off?

**Participant:** I would want to press the black screen directly—a physical, clickable screen rather than a touch-sensitive one.

---

## Stage 3: Overall Post-Test Interview

**Facilitator Statement:**

> "Thank you. You have now completed all of the tasks. I would like to ask a few final questions about your overall experience."

### Q10: Do you think Feelio could influence how or when you approach your partner?

**Participant:** Yes, it’s helpful. For example, if it says "Frustrated," I would know that I should take action to comfort them.

### Q11: Which feature felt unnecessary or too complicated?

**Participant:** None.

### Q12: If you could change one thing about Feelio, what would it be?

**Participant:** Nothing really, it's pretty cute as it is.
